Awakening stone rarities

Stone categories

A stone counts as the category of the essence it matches. Stones that match a confluence count as Confluence, and stones with no match count as Other. Require means at least one of your 16 stones must come from it.

Your own essences

Add animals, tools or anything else that exists but isn't in a confirmed combo yet. They roll like any other essence and get a matching stone.

    Differences from the original sheet's categories: Glass counts as Elemental (the Info sheet calls it an elemental sub-type), Tree is no longer an Animal, and Hammer and Knife count as both Weapon and Tool. Essences with no category are grouped as Other.
